# GTM outreach kit: messages, scripts and drafts

Ready-to-use texts for recruiting creators, testers and students, and for the public launch. Everything here matches how Chiyali works today (October 2026). If a fact changes, change it here too:

| Fact | Today |
|---|---|
| Creator's share | **70%** on sales through their own link (`?ref=`, remembered for 30 days); **50%** when Chiyali brings the buyer |
| Payments | eSewa (Khalti and Fonepay later). Prices in rupees |
| Refunds | Within 7 days and under 20% of the course watched |
| Payouts | Weekly, from NPR 1,000; earnings are held 7 days (the refund window) first; phone verification required |
| Certificates | Issued on completion, with a QR code anyone can scan to verify |
| App | Android app in closed testing on Google Play; public on Play around late October |
| Founding creators | A Founding creator badge and a spot on the home page. **A reduced fee isn't decided yet: don't promise one until it is** (see the TODO list) |

Replace anything in `[brackets]`. Keep the tone personal: these go out from you, the founder, not from "the team".

## 1. Creators

### 1.1 First message (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or WhatsApp)

**English:**
```
Hi [Name], I'm [Your name], founder of Chiyali (chiyali.com), a new course platform for Nepal. I've been following your [Loksewa / IELTS / coding] videos, and your students would get a lot out of a full course from you.

On Chiyali you get your own paid course page, students pay with eSewa, and you keep 70% of every sale that comes through your link. We set everything up for you, and your first course can even start from a YouTube playlist you already have.

Could I call you for 10 minutes this week to show you?
```

**Nepali:**
```
नमस्ते [Name] जी, म [Your name], Chiyali (chiyali.com) को संस्थापक। Chiyali नेपालका शिक्षकहरूको लागि अनलाइन कोर्स प्लेटफर्म हो। मैले तपाईंका [Loksewa / IELTS / coding] भिडियोहरू हेरेको छु, तपाईंका विद्यार्थीहरूलाई तपाईंको पूरा कोर्स निकै उपयोगी हुनेछ।

Chiyali मा तपाईंको आफ्नै कोर्स पेज हुन्छ, विद्यार्थीले eSewa बाट तिर्छन्, र तपाईंको लिंकबाट भएको हरेक बिक्रीको 70% तपाईंलाई। सबै सेटअप हामी गरिदिन्छौं, तपाईंको YouTube प्लेलिस्टबाटै पहिलो कोर्स बनाउन सकिन्छ।

```
(Have a Nepali speaker read the Nepali versions once before sending them widely.)

### 1.2 The call (about 10 minutes)

1. **Them first (2 min):** "How do you teach now? Do students already pay you for anything: classes, notes, a Telegram group?" Listen for where money already changes hands.
2. **What Chiyali is (2 min):**
   - their own course page;
   - students pay with eSewa;
   - an Android app;
   - a QR-verified certificate for every student who finishes.
3. **The money (2 min):**
   - "You keep **70%** when the student comes through your link, and 50% when Chiyali brings them.
   - Payouts are weekly, to your bank or eSewa.
   - You set the price; most courses here are NPR 499–2,999."
4. **How easy it is (2 min):**
   - "If you have a YouTube playlist, we turn it into a free course in one step. That brings in students.
   - Then you sell a deeper paid course next to it, with your recorded lessons.
   - We set up the page, the description and the thumbnail for you."
5. **The ask (2 min):**
   - "Can we have your first course up by [date]?"
   - Agree on the next step: which playlist or files, and a Google Drive link for them.
   - "Can you also test our Android app for two weeks? I just need the Gmail on your phone."

**Common questions:**
- *"What about piracy?"*
  - Paid videos are uploaded to Chiyali, not left on YouTube, and play only for students who bought them.
  - Every course is checked by us before it goes on sale.
  - There's a DMCA process for copies elsewhere.
- *"Who owns the course?"* They do. They can unpublish it anytime; students who bought it keep access.
- *"Why 50% when Chiyali brings the student?"* That covers finding the student, payments, hosting, the app and support. On their own audience, they keep 70%.

### 1.3 Follow-up after the call (WhatsApp)
```
Thanks for your time today, [Name]! As we discussed:
1. Create your teacher profile: https://www.chiyali.com/instructors/onboarding
2. Send me the [playlist link / Google Drive folder] and I'll set up the course.
3. Your target date: [date].
4. For the app test: please send the Gmail address on your Android phone.
Any question, message me here anytime.
```

### 1.4 Reminder (no reply after 3 days)
```
Hi [Name], just checking in about putting your course on Chiyali. Happy to do the setup for you: a playlist link or a Drive folder is all I need. Should I go ahead?
```

### 1.5 Onboarding checklist (you, with each creator)
- [ ] Teacher profile created, phone verified, Creator Terms accepted.
- [ ] First course: from a YouTube playlist (free) or uploaded MP4s (paid, 720p H.264 recommended).
- [ ] One **preview** lesson, a description of at least 100 characters, and a thumbnail (Claude can make one).
- [ ] Price set; course submitted; approved at `/admin/products`.
- [ ] Founding creator badge turned on at `/admin/creators`; course featured if it's strong.
- [ ] Their `?ref=` link sent to them (course page → share), plus a launch promo code if they want one.
- [ ] Payout details added (bank or eSewa) so the first payout isn't delayed.

## 3. Students and social media

### 3.1 Short video script (Reel, Short or TikTok, 30–60 seconds)

For creators to record in Nepali, using a clip from their preview lesson.

| Time | On screen | They say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Their face, bold text: the exam or skill | A hook with a question: *"Loksewa GK मा सधैं आउने ५ प्रश्न थाहा छ?"* |
| 3–25 s | A 20-second clip from the preview lesson | One real tip from the lesson (useful on its own) |
| 25–40 s | The course page on a phone | *"पूरा कोर्स Chiyali मा छ: पहिलो पाठ फ्री, eSewa बाट तिर्नुस्, सकेपछि सर्टिफिकेट।"* |
| 40–50 s | Their `?ref=` link as text, or "link in bio" | *"लिंक bio मा छ।"* |

Tips: vertical 9:16, captions on (most people watch muted), one video per week for a month.

### 3.2 Facebook group post (by the creator, in Nepali or English)
```
[Exam/skill] को तयारी गर्दै हुनुहुन्छ? मेरो "[course name]" कोर्सको पहिलो पाठ फ्रीमा हेर्नुस्: [?ref= link]
- [lessons] पाठ, [hours] घण्टा
- eSewa बाट तिर्न मिल्छ
- सकेपछि QR भएको सर्टिफिकेट
प्रश्न भए comment मा सोध्नुस्!
```
Post the free preview first, not a bare sales link; groups delete those.

### 3.3 Poster (A4 or square, for classes and groups)
- Top: the course title, large. Below: the teacher's photo and name.
- Three short lines: *"First lesson free · Pay with eSewa · Certificate with QR code"*.
- Bottom: a QR code of their `?ref=` link, with "chiyali.com" and the Chiyali logo.
- Brand blue `#0055ff`, white text, the Inter font. Claude can make the poster for each creator.
